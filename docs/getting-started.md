# Getting started

## 1. Open the app

Go to https://fomospend.xyz/app

## 2. Connect a wallet (or sign in)

Pick one:

- **Sign in with email or Google.** A Solana and a Base wallet are created for you (by Privy). Good if you don't have a crypto wallet yet.
- **Connect a Solana wallet** you already use: Phantom, Solflare, Backpack, or any wallet that supports the Solana Wallet Standard.
- **Connect a Base wallet**: MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, Rabby, or any wallet that supports EIP-6963.

You can connect both. On a phone, open the site inside your wallet app's browser.

## 3. Add USDC

Send USDC to your wallet's address:

- **Solana:** send USDC on Solana. If you have Fomo cash, it's already USDC on Solana.
- **Base:** send USDC on Base.

You don't need SOL or ETH to pay services; the seller's side covers network fees. (Sending USDC *to an agent* from your wallet is a normal transfer and needs a little SOL or ETH for the fee.)

## 4. Pick something and pay

1. Choose a service or a gift card. The app fills in the paid link for you and adds the site to your allowed list (you can undo that).
2. Fill in the one field it asks for, such as what to search for.
3. Press the button. You'll see the approval screen:
   - **Amount** in USDC and dollars
   - **Asset**: USDC and which network
   - **Destination**: who gets paid
   - **Host**: the site being paid
   - **Left today**: what remains of your daily limit after this
4. **Approve**, and confirm in your wallet. The result appears right below.

Anything outside your limits is refused with a reason, and nothing is signed.

## 5. Check your activity

The **Activity** section lists payments made from this browser, with a link to each transaction.
