# FAQ

**Do I need an account with these services?**
No. You pay for the one thing you asked for and get it back. No sign-up, API key or subscription.

**Do I need an account with Fomo Spend?**
No. Connect a wallet you already have, or sign in with email or Google if you'd like a wallet created for you.

**What does it cost?**
Whatever the service asks, shown before you approve. Most are between a tenth of a cent and a few cents. Fomo Spend adds nothing on top. Network fees for payments are covered by the seller's side.

**What's USDC?**
A digital dollar: 1 USDC is worth 1 US dollar. Fomo cash is USDC on Solana.

**Solana or Base?**
Most services take Solana, so Fomo cash works as it is. A few sellers only take Base; connect a Base wallet for those. When a seller takes both, Fomo Spend uses Solana.

**What stops it from spending too much?**
Your limits: which sites can be paid, a max per payment and a max per day. Anything outside them is refused before signing, and you approve each payment.

**A payment was refused. Why?**
The message says which limit it hit. The most common one: the site isn't on your allowed list. The app offers a one-click "Allow" for that.

**I approved but the seller didn't deliver. What now?**
The Activity list links to the transaction. The payment counts toward today's limit as soon as it's signed. Contact the seller with the transaction link.

**Can I use it with an AI agent?**
Yes. See [Agents and MCP](agents.md).
